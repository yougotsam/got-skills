# Copywriting for Marketing Pages

Write compelling marketing copy that drives conversions.

## When to Use

Homepage, landing pages, CTAs, email subject lines, ad headlines.

## Key Principles

- Lead with benefit, not feature
- Use active voice
- Short sentences (max 15 words)
- Address buyer's pain first
- Call to action must be clear

## Structure

1. Headline: Benefit or curiosity gap
2. Subheading: Clarify or expand
3. Body: Proof and social proof
4. CTA: Action-oriented, specific
